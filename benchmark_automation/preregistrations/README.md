# Historical preregistrations

The versioned directories here preserve declarative plans, receipts, thresholds,
and results as historical evidence. The v1–v10 executable validators were retired
on 2026-08-28. Their source, wheel, runner, and artifact digests remain recorded
provenance only; they do not constrain current source or re-evaluate historical
completion.

Future experiment adapters must consume a run-local implementation snapshot and
validate declared schema, authorization, result completeness, and scientific
rules without comparing those records to the current checkout.
